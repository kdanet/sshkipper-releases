Privacy

What SSHKipper sends, to whom and when. In short: none of your data.

visibility_off

Your data stays with you

None of your data: no keys, passwords, server list or AI conversations. No analytics, no crash reports.

key

License checks

A paid license sends its key and an anonymised Mac code on activation and from time to time: a subscription daily (every 5 hours in the last three days of the paid period), a lifetime license monthly, and when you open the License window while a renewal is awaited; on activation also the Mac’s name, which you see and can change.

download

Updates

The update check, if you allow it, reads a feed on sshkipper.com once a day.

cloud

Cloud drives

Signing in to Google Drive, Yandex Disk or Dropbox with SSHKipper’s own app passes the tokens to the provider through our server (which doesn’t keep them): a one-time code at sign-in, and at every renewal of the access token (about hourly for Google while the storage is in use, automations included) the refresh token, the key and the Mac code; with your own Client ID sign-in goes direct.

dns

Nothing else

The app reaches nothing else by itself: everything else is your servers, your storage and the AI provider you chose.
